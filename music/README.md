# 🎵 Background Music for Live Stream

Place your royalty-free background music tracks in this folder (`.mp3`, `.wav`, `.aac`, `.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.flac`).

## How it Works
- The stream relay will automatically detect all audio tracks in this folder and loop them continuously during your live stream.
- In `config.ini` (or the desktop GUI **Settings** dialog):
  - **`music_path = music`**: Folder or specific audio file to play.
  - **`music_mode = mix`**: Plays background music softly underneath camera microphone audio.
  - **`music_mode = fallback`**: Plays background music only when camera audio is muted or when a camera has no microphone.
  - **`music_volume = 0.3`**: Background music volume scale (0.0 to 1.0).
